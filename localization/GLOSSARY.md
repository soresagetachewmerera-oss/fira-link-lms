### Formatting Rules:
1. **Numbers**: Use standard Arabic numerals (0, 1, 2, 3...) for amounts, quantities, telephone numbers, and OTP codes.
2. **Currency**: Always append `ብር` after the formatted amount in Amharic (e.g., `1,500.00 ብር`).
3. **Punctuation**: Use Ethiopic punctuation marks where appropriate (። for periods/full stops, ፣ for commas).
4. **Placeholders**: Keep dynamic placeholders exact (`{count}`, `{name}`, etc.) and adapt sentence structure to natural Amharic SOV (Subject-Object-Verb) grammar.

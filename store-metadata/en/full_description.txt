Fira Link is Ethiopia's premier e-learning and commodity trade platform. Designed for exporters, growers, logistics operators, and international buyers.

Key Features:
- Comprehensive Masterclasses: Coffee quality grading (ECTA/SCA), export contracts, NBE directives, and transit logistics via Djibouti corridor.
- Interactive Coffee Toolkit: FOB/CIF profit margin calculator, live market prices (ICE Arabica, local floor prices), and formal FCO offer generator.
- Origin & Immersion Tours: Book verified field visits to Jimma, Yirgacheffe, Sidama, and Guji coffee washing stations and dry mills.
- Verified Certification: Earn industry-recognized credentials upon completing coursework and quizzes.
- Bilingual Experience: Full native support for English and Amharic (አማርኛ).

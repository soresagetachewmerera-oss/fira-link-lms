## About EduEx

EduEx is a Laravel-powered Learning Management System designed for academies, training centers, and course marketplaces that need a scalable, high-performing e-learning solution. It combines:

- Drag-and-drop course and event builders for instructors.
- Role-specific dashboards for admins, instructors, and students.
- Powerful analytics for enrolments, revenue, event attendance, and learner engagement.
- A comprehensive CMS (Frontend Manager) so teams can edit marketing pages without touching code.
- Multi-gateway payments, instructor payouts, and review workflows to keep revenue and quality under control.
- A polished setup wizard that guides teams through deployment in minutes.

## Core Modules

- **Courses & Events** &mdash; lessons, quizzes, assignments, downloadable resources, ticketing, and attendance tracking.
- **People & Enrolments** &mdash; instructor approvals, student directory, enrolment auditing, quiz attempts, assignment submissions.
- **Payments & Payouts** &mdash; Stripe, PayPal, Paystack, Razorpay, Flutterwave, SSLCommerz, Mollie, and offline payments plus instructor withdrawal management.
- **Reviews & Communication** &mdash; announcements, newsletters, blog posts, comments, and course reviews.
- **Certificates & Progress** &mdash; automatic certificates, completion tracking, analytics dashboards.
- **System Maintenance** &mdash; cache and queue management, system status, log viewer, maintenance mode.

## Frontend Manager

Update public-facing content visually:

- Navigation menus for desktop, mobile, footer, and quick links.
- Homepage hero, highlights, testimonials, call-to-action blocks, and featured categories.
- About, Contact, and FAQ pages with rich text editors.
- Marquee items, SEO metadata overrides, custom pages, and footer/contact information.
